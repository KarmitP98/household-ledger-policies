Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app connect to my bank account?

No. Household Ledger is completely manual by design. You never enter banking passwords, credit card numbers, or bank account credentials.

Does "settling up" transfer money?

No. Settlements in Household Ledger are records of payments you make separately (e.g. via e-Transfer, cash, or your banking app).

Can I use the app without an account?

Yes! Guest Mode allows you to use the full budgeting and bill tracking ledger locally on your phone without creating an account.

How do I invite members to my household?

Sign in to a cloud account, create a household, open the household menu, and tap "Invite member" to enter their email address.
