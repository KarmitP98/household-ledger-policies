Delete Account & Personal Data

In compliance with Google Play developer policies, Household Ledger provides both in-app and web-based deletion request pathways.

Request Deletion Online (Without Installing the App)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit your deletion request via email:

Request account and data deletion by email

Please send the request from the email address registered to your Household Ledger account so we can verify ownership. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Delete Directly Within the Android App

  1. Open Household Ledger and navigate to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down to the Danger Zone card.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm your identity using your account's password or Google sign-in.
  5. Press and hold the red Hold to Delete button.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • In-app deletion: Your Firebase Authentication account, active household membership, and user-to-household pointer are deleted.
  • Retained data: Shared household ledger records are retained and are not automatically anonymized. A deletion audit record retains your user ID, email, display name, household information, and deletion timestamps. Uploaded avatar files may also remain. These records currently have no automatic expiry.
  • Request retained-data deletion: Email the address above from your registered account address to request deletion of retained personal information. We verify ownership and process requests within 7 business days, explaining any shared records that must be retained and why.
  • Local Guest Data: Guest mode data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage deletes guest records.
