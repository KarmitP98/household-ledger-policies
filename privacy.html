Privacy Policy

Developer: Karmit Patel <karmit.patel98@gmail.com>
Hosted URL: https://karmitp98.github.io/household-ledger-policies/privacy.html

Household Ledger ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a private, clear personal and household financial record tracking utility. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, handling, and security practices across local Guest Mode and optional cloud synchronization services.

1. What We Collect

Local Guest Mode: Guest ledger records (bills, due dates, income, splits, savings goals) are stored on your device and are not synchronized to the household database. Network requests may still occur for Google or Firebase services when you use online features.

Cloud Accounts: When signing in with email or Google, we store your email address, display name, account user ID (Firebase UID), optional profile photo URL, and your household's financial tracking records (bill titles, amounts, due dates, income, expense split allocations, and savings goals).

Notifications: If you enable cloud notifications, Firebase processes an app-instance messaging token and device registration information to deliver the notifications you choose. Notification preferences and delivery records are stored with your account. Bill reminders may also be scheduled locally.

Biometric Authentication: Optional biometric unlock uses Android's local BiometricPrompt hardware keystore. The app never collects, stores, or accesses your actual fingerprint or facial biometric data.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used to deliver core app functionality: authenticating accounts, synchronizing shared ledgers with authorized household members, calculating split balances, displaying category breakdowns, scheduling bill reminders, and delivering enabled household notifications. Google Firebase provides authentication, database, storage, and messaging infrastructure.

We do not sell personal data. We do not display advertisements. We do not connect to banking APIs or scrape banking credentials.

3. Data Sharing Within Households

Records entered into a shared household are visible to other authorized members of that household. Each member retains individual privacy controls over whether their personal income is visible to the household owner.

4. Retention and Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in-app via Profile → Danger Zone → Delete account, or online without reinstalling the app via our Account Deletion Page.

In-app account deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account, active household membership, and user-to-household pointer. Shared household ledger records remain for the remaining members. These records are not automatically anonymized. A deletion audit record retains identifiers including your user ID, email, display name, household information, and deletion timestamps. Uploaded avatar files may also remain. These retained records currently have no automatic expiry.

To request deletion of retained personal information, contact us using the email registered to your account. We verify ownership and process deletion requests within 7 business days. We will explain any shared records that must be retained and the reason.

5. Security

All data in transit between the app and cloud backend is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). User authentication tokens and database access are strictly enforced via Firebase Security Rules.
